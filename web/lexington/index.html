How this was worked out, and what it doesn't mean

Every street is given a protection class from OpenStreetMap plus the city, MassDOT and MAPC facility layers. Keep only what a child can ride — paths, separated lanes, buffered lanes and quiet streets — and what's left falls into disconnected pieces. Candidate projects are the hostile streets that would join two of them, scored on the network they'd connect, how much closer they'd bring people to schools, playgrounds and libraries, recorded bike crashes, and residents who'd gain a safe route at all.
